# Market data sources

- [NerkhAra public JSON](https://nerkhara.com/en/api/) provides /api/v1/latest.json and /api/v1/latest/{id}.json. Check unit, updated_utc, as_of, and stale for each observation. Its [market page](https://nerkhara.com/en/) is a fallback when JSON cannot be retrieved. The FX cross rates are derived from USD and global rates. For crypto, prefer the local price_irt field when present and confirm its scale against the published market page or schema. The primary crypto price can be quoted in USDT.
- [AlanChand API](https://alanchand.com/en/media/api) provides direct Tehran market prices for all 12 fiat codes when a token is available. [Bonbast API](https://www.bon-bast.com/docs) is a cross-check for the open-market dollar. Label direct buy/sell or derived rates as applicable and keep the rate side consistent.
- [Nobitex public market data](https://github.com/nobitex/docs-api/blob/master/source/includes/_market_data.md) supports /market/stats with dstCurrency=rls. Use [CoinGecko simple price](https://docs.coingecko.com/reference/simple-price) for worldwide USD quotes when requested. The five IDs are bitcoin, ethereum, tether, solana, tron.
- [Gold API](https://gold-api.com/faqs) documents public spot endpoints /price/XAU and /price/XAG. [LBMA](https://www.lbma.org.uk/prices-and-data/about-lbma-daily-auction-prices) supplies delayed benchmarks. For Iranian 18K use NerkhAra gold-18k or an equivalent documented local quote.
- [EIA spot series](https://www.eia.gov/opendata/browser/petroleum/pri/spt) supplies Brent RBRTE and WTI RWTC in USD per barrel. The [public spot table](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/prices/) is a fallback. NerkhAra publishes opec-basket with its observation date. The [World Bank Pink Sheet](https://www.worldbank.org/en/research/commodity-markets) supplies monthly Dubai crude.

- For extra local rows, use NerkhAra IDs `aed`, `chf`, `gold-24k`, `gold-mesghal`, `coin-emami`, `coin-bahar-azadi`, `coin-half`, `coin-quarter`, and `coin-gram`. Label the provider's rate basis for AED and CHF. Use the quoted mesghal and per-piece prices directly.
- For XRP, BNB, DOGE and PAXG use NerkhAra IDs `xrp`, `bnb`, `doge`, and `paxg`. Prefer a verified local IRT observation. For a worldwide USD quote use CoinGecko IDs `ripple`, `binancecoin`, `dogecoin`, and `pax-gold`. Label international prices and any conversion basis explicitly. Keep USDT-denominated observations distinct from USD observations.
- [LBMA platinum and palladium](https://www.lbma.org.uk/prices-and-data/lbma-platinum-and-palladium-price) publishes delayed London auction benchmarks in USD per troy ounce. Use the latest published USD observation and identify its date and auction session.
- [EIA Henry Hub daily spot](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/ng/hist/rngwhhdd.htm) publishes USD per million BTU. Use the latest dated observation.
- [World Bank Pink Sheet](https://www.worldbank.org/en/research/commodity-markets) publishes monthly copper and aluminum averages in USD per metric ton. Show the observation month and mark the quote as a monthly average.
