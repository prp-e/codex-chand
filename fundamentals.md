# Fundamental analysis

Write an independent, evidence-based assessment in the requested language. For dashboard chart submissions, add a separate fundamental section after the technical analysis. For direct requests, follow the requested scope and include the latest verified quote only when it helps explain valuation or market context.

Verify current figures, releases, and events using primary sources. Cite the relevant release, filing, or data series near each material claim. Date the assessment and distinguish the reporting period, publication date, and market observation date. For a historical assessment, use information available by the end of the selected period. Identify any later information explicitly if the user asks for hindsight. Align currencies, periods, and instrument definitions before comparing figures.

Choose the drivers that matter for the specific instrument.

- **Fiat.** Use inflation, real-rate or policy differentials, external balances, and dated policy changes as relevant. For USD/Tehran toman and related crosses, distinguish the Tehran open-market rate from official and administered rates. Check Iranian inflation, currency policy, access to hard currency, and relevant oil or sanctions developments. Attribute global USD strength and local toman weakness separately when evidence supports a distinction.
- **Crypto.** Use relevant network activity, issuance or unlocks, liquidity, and verified protocol events. For stablecoins and tokenized gold, consider backing, attestations, redemption, and peg behavior. For local quotes, distinguish global asset moves from the Tehran FX or local venue premium.
- **Metals, energy, and Iranian gold coins.** Use supply, demand, inventories, and dated production or policy releases as relevant. For precious metals, consider real yields, the global dollar, and demand evidence. For Iranian gold and coins, distinguish the world metal price, Tehran FX and the local premium. Estimate a coin premium only with verified specifications and aligned gold and FX observations.
- **Equities, indices, and funds.** Use issuer or exchange filings, including Codal for Iranian listings, and comparable reporting periods. Focus on earnings, margins, cash flow, balance sheet, and valuation where supported. For indices, use constituent exposure and aggregate measures with documented methodology. For funds, distinguish the traded unit price from dated NAV and check holdings or benchmark exposure.

Explain the likely drivers, the evidence supporting them, and the strongest credible counterfactor or uncertainty. Distinguish verified facts from AI interpretation. When tying a driver to a price move, check timing, direction, and plausible alternatives. Use conditional language where attribution is uncertain and identify what new evidence would change the assessment. Where material inputs are unavailable, state the gap and qualify the conclusion. Keep scenarios conditional and avoid unsupported price targets.
